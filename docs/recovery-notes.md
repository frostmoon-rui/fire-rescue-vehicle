# Recovery scope

The public archive contains the recovered ESP32 camera sketch and its local source dependencies, two DXF drawings, and an English technical transcription of the IR button mapping. Existing source structure and known limitations are documented rather than presented as newly verified functionality.

Private network notes, local credential backups, logs, office documents, presentations, serial-debugger executables, unreviewed photographs and videos, binary CAD backups and unrelated reference material are not published. Original local project files remain unchanged.

The DXF copies use English filenames and layout names. Their last-saved-by header fields are replaced with a neutral project label. The drawings have not been dimensionally checked against the physical vehicle; retain an independent mechanical review before fabrication.

Public text is maintained in English. No personal appendices, participant identities, private contact information or competition paperwork belong in this repository.
